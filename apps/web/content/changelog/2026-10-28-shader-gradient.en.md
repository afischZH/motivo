**New: Shader Gradient.**

- A gradient computed by a shader right on your graphics card: noise with domain warping runs through two to six colors.
- Seven styles: Liquid, Silk, Streaked, Grainy, Marble, Ripple and Aurora. Frequency, contrast, warp, detail, angle and zoom shape the field.
- Surface with grain, vignette and Bayer dithering (2×2, 4×4 or 8×8) with adjustable color steps and cell size.
- The preview moves in a seamless loop unless you have turned on reduced motion in your system. The moment slider picks the still frame for exports.
- Export as PNG, JPG and WebP up to 8K, rendered in tiles and pixel for pixel like the preview, plus a seamless GIF loop.
- Without WebGL2 the editor shows a notice instead of an empty area.
