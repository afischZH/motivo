title: Four new pattern generators
tools: [grid-patterns, confetti, starry-sky, dieter-dots]
---

- **Grid Patterns**: grid lines, dot grids, crosses or tiles, with a major grid, dashed or dotted lines and a soft fade towards the center or the edges.
- **Confetti**: strips, circles, triangles and squiggles, evenly spread with Poisson-disk sampling, with rotation and size variation.
- **Starry Sky**: a night sky with a color gradient, stars of varying size and brightness, optional glow and sparkles.
- **Dieter Dots**: concentric rings of dots like a speaker grille, with a twist into a spiral, shrinking towards the edge and color gradients.

All four run entirely in your browser and export as SVG, CSS, PNG, JPG and WebP.
