title: "New tool: Noise and Grain"
tools: [noise-grain]
---

**Noise and Grain** creates grainy textures and backgrounds: fine Gaussian noise over a color, a linear or radial gradient, or on its own as a transparent grain layer. Adjust amount, grain size, blend mode (overlay, soft light, multiply) and monochrome or color grain. Export as PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG or CSS. The grain is computed from the seed and looks the same in preview and export.
