**The generators and the social editor share a calmer, consistent layout.**

- The tool name sits in the middle of the header. Click it to search the tools and jump straight to the next one.
- The side panels are made of collapsible sections with icons. Presets and canvas size are on the left, settings on the right.
- The round arrows at the edges of the canvas hide the side panels to give your design more room.
- The canvas shows your design on a sheet over a fine dot grid. Zoom in and out at the bottom left, fit it to the window or jump to 100%.
- In the social editor, format, add and layers are on the left. Export opens a small menu with file format and size; more actions sit behind the three dots.
