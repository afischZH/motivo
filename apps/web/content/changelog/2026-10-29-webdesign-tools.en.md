**New: fluid type scale, section dividers and Open Graph images.**

- The fluid type scale works out every font size of your website between phone and desktop, as CSS clamp() with CSS variables, classes and a Tailwind theme. A hint warns when text might stay too small when zoomed.
- The section divider generator draws waves, arcs, slants, peaks, arrows, clouds and steps as the transition between two sections. The SVG fits any width, and the HTML example comes with a height that grows along.
- The Open Graph image generator designs preview images for shared links with a title, logo and site name on a Musterli motif or your own picture, plus meta tags to copy.
