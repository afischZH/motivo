**Musterli now works fully from the keyboard.**

- Press ⌘K or Ctrl+K anywhere to open the command palette: find a tool, open a page, or in the editor export, roll a new variant or apply your brand palette.
- Star your favorite tools. They show up with your recently used tools at the top of the catalogue and in the palette, stored only in your browser.
- “In context” shows your motif as the background of a website with a title, text and button, including its WCAG contrast.
- New in the catalogue: tools by task, such as hero backgrounds, surface patterns, UI effects or color systems.
- Social template thumbnails load more smoothly because the patterns are computed in the background.
