**Musterli now looks like a tool for web design.**

- The home page shows a wall of real motifs from the generators.
- In the editor, settings sit in compact rows, label on the left, value on the right.
- Drag number fields sideways with the mouse to change them, with Shift for bigger steps.
- The preview sits on a stage with a fine dot grid, almost black in dark mode.
- In the catalogue, motifs fill the cards edge to edge.
