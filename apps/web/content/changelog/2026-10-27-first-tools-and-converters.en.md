**Musterli launches with four generators.**

- **CSS Gradient**: linear, radial and conic gradients with smooth OKLCH transitions.
- **SVG Waves**: layered waves for sections, headers and backgrounds.
- **Blob Generator**: organic shapes in one click, filled, outlined or with a gradient.
- **Polka Dots**: dot patterns on a grid, offset or freely scattered.

Press Space for a new variant, lock single values, undo, and share a link. Export as SVG, CSS, PNG, JPG and WebP, all right in your browser.

Plus six **converters**: SVG to CSS, CSS to SVG, SVG to Base64, Base64 to SVG, image to Base64 and Base64 to image. Your files never leave the browser.
