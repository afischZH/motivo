**New: tiles with text, an icon and pictures, plus your own background picture for Glass and Border Effects.**

- Bento grid: click a tile to open "Edit tile". Add a title and subtitle, pick an icon from the shape library, upload your own graphic and a background picture per tile. You can also set a background picture for the whole grid.
- The code export includes your text and icons; pictures appear as placeholders with a note. SVG and image exports show everything.
- Glass and border effects: upload your own photo or SVG as the stage, then choose fill or show all and the position.
- Your files stay on your device: they are never in a share link, but saved designs keep them.
