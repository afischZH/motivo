**New: three trend looks as their own tools.**

- The holographic gradient makes iridescent foil with shifting rainbow bands, folds and sheen, from pearl to oil slick.
- Neon shapes draws glowing circles, polygons, lines and waves on a dark background, with a white core and soft haze. The glow needs no filter, so the SVG stays editable.
- The glitch background tears a gradient into shifted slices with RGB split, blocks and scanlines. As a GIF it flickers like a broken signal.
