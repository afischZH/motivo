title: Three new background generators
tools: [blob-composition, doodles, svg-charts]
---

- **Blob Composition**: several blobs as layers at corners, edges or the center. Positions and sizes are stored relative, so the composition adapts to any format.
- **Doodle Backgrounds**: squiggles, zigzags, spirals, loops, triangles, stars, crosses and arcs that look drawn by hand. With adjustable wobble, evenly spread with Poisson-disk sampling.
- **SVG Charts**: placeholder charts with random data for mockups, as line, area or bars, with smoothing, axes and grid lines.

All three run entirely in your browser and export as SVG, CSS, PNG, JPG and WebP.
