**New in Seamless Patterns: three more motif sources.**

- Logo patterns: your logo from the Brand Kit is now right in the shape picker. With Alternate, every other row or checkerboard cell turns upside down, like a logo wall.
- Emoji patterns: type up to eight emoji and Musterli spreads them over the tile at random. You can also color them with your motif colors.
- Text patterns: a word, a name or a monogram becomes a repeat, in four font styles.
