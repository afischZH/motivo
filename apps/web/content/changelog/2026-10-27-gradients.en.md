title: "New tools: Mesh Gradient, Blurry Gradient and Striped Gradient"
tools: [mesh-gradient, blurry-gradient, striped-gradient]
---

Three new gradient generators. **Mesh Gradient** blends three to eight blurred color fields, with adjustable warp, blur and grain. **Blurry Gradient** places two to five large, soft circles in the corners or around the center; every circle is an exactly computed radial gradient, so the SVG needs no filter. **Striped Gradient** splits a gradient into vertical stripes with a slight offset, optionally shaded like fluted glass. All three export as PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG or CSS, and preview and export look the same.
