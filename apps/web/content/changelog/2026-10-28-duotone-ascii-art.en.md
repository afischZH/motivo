**New: Duotone Generator.**

- Tints a photo with two, three or any number of colors: shadows take the first color, highlights the last, with a smooth blend in between.
- Brightness, contrast and gamma, plus optional film grain and a mix with the original.
- With a brand palette, the photo takes your brand colors, sorted from dark to light.
- Without your own photo, the editor shows a sample picture drawn from scratch. Export as PNG, JPG and WebP at full resolution.

**New: ASCII Art Generator.**

- Turns a photo into characters, colored characters, braille dots or an LED dot matrix with round or square dots.
- Columns, character aspect ratio, character ramp (including your own), invert, colors and background are adjustable.
- Export as SVG, PNG, JPG, WebP, as a text file and as HTML with a `pre` block.

For both: your photo stays on your device. It is processed in the browser, never uploaded and not shared in links. The preview renders in the background, so the editor stays smooth even with large phone photos.
