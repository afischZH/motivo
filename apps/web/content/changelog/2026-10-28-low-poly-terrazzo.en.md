**New: Low Poly & Voronoi.**

- Faceted backgrounds made of triangles (Delaunay) or organic Voronoi cells, over naturally spread points or a slightly jittered grid. The faces always cover the whole image.
- Every face takes its color from a linear, radial or mesh gradient through your palette. Color variation and lighting with an adjustable direction add depth.
- Lines for a wireframe look, gaps and rounded corners for mosaic tiles.
- Export as SVG, CSS, PNG, JPG, WebP and GIF, including slowly drifting points.

**New: Terrazzo Pattern.**

- Angular chips, pebbles or both in two or three size classes, spread without overlap, plus speckles and fine grain.
- Color weights through the main color share, brand palettes that keep the base color.
- A seamless tile by default: chips at the edge continue on the opposite side. Export as CSS background, PNG tile, SVG, PNG, JPG, WebP and GIF.
