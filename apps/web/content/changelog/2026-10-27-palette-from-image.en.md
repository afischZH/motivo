**New: Palette from Image.**

- Drop a photo into the editor, paste it with Ctrl+V or choose a file (PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF or SVG up to 5 MB).
- Motivo finds 3 to 10 main colors with k-means in the OKLab color space and shows how much area each color covers.
- Press the space bar for another grouping, sort by share, lightness or hue.
- Export as a HEX list, CSS variables, `tailwind.config.js`, Tailwind v4 `@theme` or an image.
- Your image stays in your browser: it is never uploaded, never stored and never part of shared links.
