title: Halftone, CMYK Halftone and Dithering
tools: [halftone, cmyk-halftone, dither]
---

**New: Halftone Effect.**

- Splits a photo into a rotated grid of dots, lines, squares or diamonds: the darker a spot, the larger the shape.
- Cell size, screen angle, ink and paper (also transparent) are up to you; invert turns the light areas into dots.
- Export as PNG, JPG, WebP and as SVG with real vector shapes, for plotters or screen printing.

**New: CMYK Halftone.**

- Separates the photo into cyan, magenta, yellow and black, each ink with its own screen angle like offset printing (15, 75, 0 and 45 degrees, all adjustable).
- Gray component replacement, misregistration for the look of a slipped print, your own inks and paper.
- Export as PNG, JPG, WebP and SVG, with the inks in multiply blend mode.

**New: Dither Effect.**

- Floyd-Steinberg, Atkinson, Stucki and Bayer patterns (2, 4 and 8).
- Palettes: 1-bit with two colors of your choice, four grays, four greens, the four colors of early PC graphics or your own colors (also from the Brand Kit).
- Pixel size for coarser dots. The pattern is always computed on the same grid, so preview and export look the same.

For all three: your photo stays on your device. It is processed in the browser, never uploaded and not shared in links.
