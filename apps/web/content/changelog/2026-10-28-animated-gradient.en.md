**New: Animated Gradient Generator.**

- Four kinds of motion as pure CSS: a panning gradient, a rotating conic or linear gradient, colors that take turns, and soft blobs floating over a base color.
- Set duration, easing, direction, motion, blur and intensity; Space rolls new colors and a new blob layout.
- People who prefer reduced motion see a calm, still gradient. Every code export includes the matching prefers-reduced-motion rule.
- Export as CSS, HTML example, Tailwind classes and Tailwind v4 @theme, as a still image in PNG, JPG, WebP and SVG, and as a seamless GIF loop.
