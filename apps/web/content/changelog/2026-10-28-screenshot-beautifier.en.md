**New: Screenshot Beautifier.**

- Drop a screenshot into the editor, choose a file or paste it with Ctrl+V (PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG up to 5 MB). The picture stays in your browser and never goes into a share link; neither does the address in the bar.
- Padding, alignment, rounded corners, a soft multi-layer shadow, border and light edge. Plus a self-drawn window or browser bar in light or dark with your own address.
- Background as a color, a gradient or a preset of Mesh, Blurry, Striped, Waves, Blob Composition, Starry Sky and Grid, in the preset's colors or your palette. Space rolls only the background.
- Ready sizes for Product Hunt, X, LinkedIn and documentation. Export as PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG with the picture embedded.
