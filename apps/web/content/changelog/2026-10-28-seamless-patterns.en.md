**New: Seamless Patterns.**

- 24 original shapes, from circle and heart to leaf and wave, tiled seamlessly: motifs at the border reappear on the opposite side.
- Four tilings: grid, brick, half drop and mirror. Plus size, spacing, fixed and random rotation, size variation and offset.
- Your own graphic as the motif: load an SVG, PNG, JPG or WebP and crop it (free or square, keyboard included). Recolor it in one color or keep the original colors.
- Motivo checks SVG files strictly and removes scripts, embedded web content and external references.
- Export as SVG with a pattern tile, CSS background, a single PNG tile or an image (PNG, JPG, WebP).
- Your file stays in your browser: it is never uploaded, never stored and never part of shared links.
