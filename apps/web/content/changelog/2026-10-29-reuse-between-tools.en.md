**New: send a design to the next tool in one click.**

- "Reuse" sends your result as an image to another tool, for example a gradient to the Screenshot Beautifier or a dot pattern to the Halftone effect.
- The image stays in your browser. It never goes into a share link or onto a server.
- From the colour tools, "Save as brand palette" stores a palette straight in your Brand Kit, ready in every generator.
- In "My designs", designs from the shader gradient and the image effects now have a thumbnail too.
