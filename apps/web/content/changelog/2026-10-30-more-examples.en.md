**Every tool in these categories now has four more presets.**

- Gradients: for example Aurora, Peach, Galaxy, Deep sea and a dithered mono shader.
- CSS patterns: terracotta scales, Notebook, Mint check and Art deco.
- Image effects: Risograph, Blueprint, Vaporwave, 8-bit, Underwater, LED matrix and more.
- Colors: new palettes, scales and contrast examples such as Nordic, Teal or Red-green.
- CSS effects and layouts: Hairline, Glow, Rainbow frame, Hills, Golden ratio and new bento grids.
- Tool pages now show eight presets instead of four, each opens in the editor with one click.
