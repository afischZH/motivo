**Improved: catalogue and navigation.**

- The catalogue has a search that filters as you type, with or without accents. Press `/` to jump into the search field, `Esc` to clear it.
- The search field and categories stay visible while you scroll, as a swipeable row on phones.
- The main menu shows where you are. On narrow screens it sits behind a menu button instead of crowding the header.
- The categories on the home page sit in a calm three-column grid.
