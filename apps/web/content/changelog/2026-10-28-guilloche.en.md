**New: Guilloche.**

- Fine interlaced line work like on banknotes, certificates and vouchers, from well-known formulas: weave (lines swinging between two edges), spirograph (rotating pointers) and harmonic (two overlaid sine waves).
- Three layouts: rosette, band (tiles seamlessly left to right, ideal as a border) and a frame around the image with rounded corners.
- Set the number of lines, petals, swings, depth, offset, inner and outer edge and line width, plus a second layer in its own color.
- Colors: one color, alternating, a blend across the lines, an iris print across the image or a blend along every line. Brand palettes work as everywhere.
- Export as SVG (compact, typically 50 to 250 KB), PNG, JPG, WebP and as a GIF in which the lines flow.
