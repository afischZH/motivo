**New: four animated backgrounds that run on your graphics card.**

- **Dot Wave Grid:** a grid of dots, squares, dashes, crosses or rings that swell with a wave or noise field.
- **Liquid Chrome:** molten metal with reflections and highlights, from mercury and gold to an oil-slick rainbow.
- **Caustics & Cells:** shimmering pool light, organic cells or crystal facets.
- **Light Beams:** soft rays from a light source you place anywhere, even outside the picture.
- Like the shader gradient, all four export PNG, JPG and WebP up to 8K, a seamless GIF loop and live code for your website (Web Component, React, HTML and GLSL).
