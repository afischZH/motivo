**Improved: the settings panel in every generator.**

- A reset button appears next to every value you have changed. "Reset all" at the top of the panel sets everything back to the defaults, undoable as usual with ⌘/Ctrl+Z.
- Long explanations sit behind an info button next to the name, short ones stay visible.
- The locks that keep a value when shuffling appear when you hover a row. Locked values always show their lock, in the accent colour. On touch devices every lock stays visible.
- Long option names wrap instead of being cut off. Number fields share one width, with the unit inside the field.
