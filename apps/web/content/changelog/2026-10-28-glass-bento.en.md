**New: Glass and Border Effects and Bento Grid.**

- Glass and border effects: frosted glass with blur, tint, saturation, a light inner border and grain, gradient borders (rotating if you like) and colored glows. The preview is real CSS on a Musterli background of your choice.
- The glass code includes the prefix Safari needs, and the rotating border stands still when you asked your system for less motion.
- A hint appears when text on the glass misses WCAG AA contrast.
- Export as CSS, Tailwind classes or an HTML example; gradient borders and glows also as PNG or WebP.
- Bento grid: rounded tiles of different sizes fill a grid without gaps. Space rolls a new layout, the lock keeps it.
- Export as CSS Grid with HTML (including a layout for narrow screens), Tailwind classes, SVG or an image.
