**New: website mockups in the Screenshot Beautifier.**

- Show your website on a laptop, monitor, tablet or phone, or as a responsive mockup on two to four devices at once: staggered, in a row or on a stage. The devices are drawn from scratch, in silver, graphite or white, the phone with an island, a notch or neither.
- One screenshot is enough: every device shows it, cropped to its screen from the top. You can add separate screenshots for tablet and phone. Without a picture the devices show a placeholder website.
- The address appears in the browser bar of laptop and monitor. With the live view your browser loads the website right into the devices, and “Take this view” turns it into pictures (Chrome). If a website does not allow embedding, upload a screenshot.
- Six new presets and the sizes Dribbble (1600 × 1200) and square. Pictures and address stay in your browser and never go into a share link.
