**New: three tools for shapes and stickers.**

- The starburst badge generator makes price tags, seals and stickers with a spiky, scalloped or wavy edge, your own text, a ring, a sticker border and a hard shadow. As a GIF the edge turns.
- Squiggle lines draws waves, zigzags, loops and scribbles as one line, stacked or scattered like confetti. It also gives you a squiggly underline in CSS.
- The Bauhaus pattern fills a grid with quarter circles, half circles, rings and triangles in bold flat colors. Space deals a new arrangement.
