**New: three color tools.**

- **Color Palette Generator**: press Space for a new palette, lock single colors, harmonies (analogous, complementary, triadic, split complementary, tetradic) in OKLCH, WCAG contrast and a contrast matrix.
- **Tints & Shades**: lighter and darker steps of a color, exported as HEX, RGB, HSL or CSS variables.
- **Tailwind Color Palette**: eleven steps from 50 to 950 with your color on 500, exported for `tailwind.config.js` and Tailwind v4 `@theme`.
