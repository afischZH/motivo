With **CSS Patterns** you design backgrounds in pure CSS, no image file needed. Choose from 34 original patterns in five categories: dots, grids, stripes, waves and geometric shapes, from gingham to chevron. Colors, size, opacity and, for stripes and grids, the angle are adjustable. Export as CSS, Tailwind classes, SVG, PNG, JPG or WebP. Every pattern also has its own page with a live preview and code to copy, listed under CSS Patterns.
