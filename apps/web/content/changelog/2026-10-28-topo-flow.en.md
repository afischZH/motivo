title: Topographic Lines and Flow Fields
tools: [topo-lines, flow-field]
---

**New: Topographic Lines.**

- A random terrain from noise with adjustable scale, detail levels, roughness and warp, drawn as contour lines at even intervals.
- Lines only, filled elevation bands or both. Every nth line can be highlighted as a wider index line, like on a map.
- Colors run from low to high or alternate. With a brand palette, the line color automatically takes your darkest brand color.
- Export as SVG (simplified and rounded, at most about 1.5 MB), PNG, JPG, WebP and as a GIF in which the lines slowly move.

**New: Flow Fields.**

- Lines follow a field of noise or swirls. Start points evenly spread, on a grid or random.
- A minimum distance keeps lines from clumping. At 0 they may overlap, for soft textures like silk or hair.
- Even, tapering or pointed strokes. Colors at random from the palette, by direction or by position.
- Export as SVG, PNG, JPG, WebP and as a GIF with strokes that travel along the field.
