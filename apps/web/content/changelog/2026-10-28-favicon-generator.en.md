**New: Favicon Generator.**

- Favicons and app icons from a letter, a shape from the library or your own logo (SVG or image, it stays on your device).
- Solid, gradient, pattern or transparent background. Square, rounded, squircle or circle tile with adjustable padding.
- Own colors for dark mode: favicon.svg then switches by itself when the browser uses a dark theme.
- Preview the standard, dark, Apple touch and maskable variants, including the safe zone.

**New: Icon pack as a ZIP.**

- One click gives you favicon.ico (16, 32 and 48 px), favicon.svg, the Apple touch icon, app icons at 192 and 512 px, a maskable icon and site.webmanifest.
