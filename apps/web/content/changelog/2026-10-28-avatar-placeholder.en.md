**New: Avatar Generator.**

- Profile pictures with initials (1 to 3 letters), as a symmetric identicon or as a composition of shapes.
- Color and pattern follow from the name: same name, same avatar, on every device. Or let chance decide, then the space bar brings new identicons.
- Circle, rounded or square, filled with a solid color, a gradient or a pattern from the CSS pattern library. System fonts only, no font files.
- Export as SVG, PNG, JPG and WebP at 128, 256, 512 or 1024 pixels.

**New: Placeholder Image Generator.**

- Placeholders in any size with width × height, your own text or no label. The text adapts to narrow formats.
- Solid color, gradient or pattern, plus diagonals and a border. Ad formats such as 300 × 250 and 728 × 90 are in the size list.
- Export as SVG, PNG, JPG and WebP.
