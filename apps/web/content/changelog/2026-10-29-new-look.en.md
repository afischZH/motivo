**New: a fresh look across the whole website.**

- The header now stays at the top while you scroll, with a new navigation bar. On phones the menu opens as a side panel.
- Home, catalogue, categories and collections share one page header, larger previews and cards that lift slightly on hover.
- The questions on the home page now fold open one by one.
- In the editor, fields, dropdowns and sliders look calmer. Every feature and shortcut stays the same, and so do your saved designs and share links.
