**New: write your own text on the card in Glass and Border Effects.**

- In the new "Text" group, enter a title and a short text and choose whether it sits on the left or in the centre.
- The preview, the image export and the HTML example show your text. The contrast hint still checks the text colour.
- Your text stays when you switch presets or press Space, and it travels in the share link.
- Without your own text, the card shows neutral placeholder bars as before.
