**New: two collections to browse.**

- 36 gradients and 36 color palettes, sorted by mood (such as warm, cool, earthy or pastel).
- Every page lists the colors in HEX, RGB and OKLCH, and each value copies with one click.
- Gradients come as ready-made CSS and a Tailwind class, palettes as CSS variables and a Tailwind theme.
- One click opens the colors in the editor: as a CSS gradient, a mesh or blurry background, a palette, tints and shades or a Tailwind palette.
- Every gradient and palette is our own selection and free to use.
