title: God Rays, Fractal Glass and Pixelate
tools: [god-rays, fractal-glass, pixelate]
---

**New: God Rays Effect.**

- Lets light from a source you place pour through your photo; dark shapes such as mountains or branches cast streaks of shadow.
- Threshold for bright areas, strength, ray length, decay, ray color and two blend modes. The light may also sit outside the picture.

**New: Fractal Glass Effect.**

- Puts your photo behind reeded glass: strips at any width and angle, flat, rounded or ridged.
- Distortion, mirroring, a random offset per strip, frosted blur, gloss and glass color.

**New: Pixelate Image.**

- Big pixels as the mean of their area, plus posterizing to levels per channel or to your palette (also from the Brand Kit).
- Export also as SVG with one rectangle per run of pixels, for example as a pattern for mosaics or cross-stitch.

For all three: your photo stays on your device. It is processed in the browser, never uploaded and not shared in links.
