**New: clip-path generator, QR code generator and image compressor.**

- The clip-path generator cuts images and sections into polygons, stars, blobs, circles, wave edges, slants, arrows, cut corners and speech bubbles. Copy the CSS in percent, an SVG clipPath for smooth curves or the Tailwind class. Your own picture stays on your device.
- The new image compressor makes PNG, JPG, WebP and GIF smaller and converts them to WebP, JPG or PNG, optionally with a smaller edge. Up to 30 images at once, download one by one or as a ZIP. Everything runs in your browser.
- The QR code generator designs QR codes with dots, rounded modules, custom eyes, gradients and your logo. Our own encoder runs in your browser; the code never expires and there is no redirect.
