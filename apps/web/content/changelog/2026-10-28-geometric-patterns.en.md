**New: Geometric Patterns, our flagship tool.**

- Grids of tiles with shapes from our own shape library: a whole pack or your own selection.
- Even grids or nested mosaics where tiles split into smaller ones, up to four levels deep.
- Quarter turns (for Truchet mazes from the “Tiles and quarters” pack) or free rotation, colors per tile or per shape layer, tile fills, a second layer with its own opacity, and outlines instead of fills.
- Edit single tiles: click a tile, or use Tab, the arrow keys and Enter. Swap shape, color and rotation; every change can be undone and travels with share links and saved designs.
- Export as SVG, a seamlessly repeating CSS background, PNG, JPG or WebP.
